IMPORTS: Connections > blank template or authorised Arbor CSV > Match columns > Preview > Import. Use Student Name, School Email, Classes, Year; Student ID is optional but recommended. Choose the import year or use each row's year. Conflicting details stop the import. Memberships default to add; replacement is explicit. New classes require teacher assignment.
MEMBERSHIPS: People & classes > select class > add, remove or move selected pupils. Choose multiple teachers. Clearing all teacher boxes restricts that class to administrators. Invite staff through School settings > School setup. Manually edited OneHome memberships persist through linked MIS refreshes.
GRADUATION: Select actual Year 11 leavers, then type GRADUATE YEAR 11. Their OneHome access is disabled and their results, points and memberships are preserved. Restore explicitly if needed. Re-import and MIS sync cannot silently restore graduates.

BUDDIES: 24 free companions including Panda, Polar Bear, Dog, Cat, Cow and Highland Cow. Name, care and outfit ownership carry over. School disable controls still apply.

Text-recall checkpoints are automatically prepared from passages; school staff should review suitability, historic language and themes. Sources are in BOOK-SOURCES.txt.
ADVENT: Automatically available on UK server dates 1–25 December. Only today's door opens, once per pupil per year. Days 1–24 randomly award 5–25 Star or Power Points per pupil. Gifts never count towards homework targets. Door 25 awards a homework pass. Students select published homework and request teacher approval; approval applies only to that student's assignment. Declined passes remain available for another request. School settings can disable advent. The isolated demo follows the browser date; live decisions use server time only.
